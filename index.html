Practice
An independent consultancy between Madrid and Mexico City — treating ecology, heritage and finance as load-bearing inputs to the design of infrastructure.

Iván Tamayo Ramos is an architect and regenerative-design consultant working between infrastructure, ecological restoration and sustainable finance.

He currently advises ACCIONA on the integration of regenerative principles across major infrastructure tenders in Europe, the Americas and Australasia, while developing an AI-assisted tool for regenerative infrastructure across the project lifecycle. A 2024 Fellow of the Norman Foster Institute, with more than a decade of built work across Mexico, Spain and Central Europe.
